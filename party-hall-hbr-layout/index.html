A party hall for every celebration

AFSON Convention Centre is not only for weddings. Families book the hall for birthday parties, anniversaries, baby showers, farewell parties and community get-togethers. The air-conditioned hall has a raised stage, and in-house decoration and DJ are available.

The hall is large, so it works best for bigger gatherings. Guests can park in the basement or along the 80 ft road, and the dining area serves 200 at a time.

Questions people ask

Can I book the hall for a birthday party?

Yes. The hall is available for birthdays, anniversaries and other family celebrations, with in-house decor and DJ if you want them.

Is the party hall air-conditioned?

Yes, the hall is air-conditioned.

Where is AFSON Convention Centre?

23, 1st Main Rd, HBR Layout 2nd Block, 1st Stage, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560043. It is on the 1st Main Road (80 ft road) in HBR Layout, North Bengaluru.

How many guests does the hall hold?

The air-conditioned hall seats up to 900 guests and the dining area serves 200 at a time. A terrace gives separate vegetarian dining.

Is there parking?

Yes. Basement parking for around 25 cars and 70 two-wheelers, plus ample parking along the 80 ft road in front.

How do I check if my date is free?

Call or WhatsApp 94804 47789 with your date and approximate guest count and we will confirm availability.
