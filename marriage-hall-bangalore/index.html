What our marriage hall offers

Choosing a marriage hall in Bangalore comes down to a few practical things: how many guests it holds, whether your family can reach it easily, whether parking works for the day, and whether you can use the caterer you trust. AFSON Convention Centre was built around those questions.

The hall is on the 1st Main Road (80 ft road) in HBR Layout, in North Bengaluru, close to Kalyan Nagar, Kammanahalli, Banaswadi and Hennur. The main hall is air-conditioned and seats up to 900 guests. The dining area serves 200 at a time, and a terrace gives separate vegetarian dining.
